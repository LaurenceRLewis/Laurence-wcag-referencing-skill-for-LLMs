# SC 2.2.2 — Pause, Stop, Hide (Level A)

## Intent
Moving, blinking, scrolling, or auto-updating content that starts automatically and lasts more than 5 seconds must have a pause, stop, or hide mechanism — so users who find moving content distracting or disorienting can control it.

## Applies to
Carousels, animated banners, auto-scrolling content, ticker feeds, parallax effects, animated backgrounds.

## Passes when
- A pause/stop/hide control is provided for any auto-moving content lasting more than 5 seconds, OR
- The movement lasts 5 seconds or less and stops automatically, OR
- The movement is essential to the content (e.g. a progress indicator)

## Fails when
- An auto-rotating carousel has no pause button
- A scrolling news ticker runs indefinitely with no stop control
- Animated background content has no way to stop it

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
