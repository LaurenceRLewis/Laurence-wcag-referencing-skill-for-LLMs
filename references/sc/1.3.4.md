# SC 1.3.4 — Orientation (Level AA)

## Intent
Content does not restrict its view and operation to a single display orientation — portrait or landscape — unless a specific orientation is essential to the function.

## Applies to
Any web content or application that locks orientation via CSS or JavaScript.

## Passes when
- The page works correctly in both portrait and landscape orientations
- If orientation is locked, there is a clear essential reason (e.g. a piano keyboard app that requires landscape)

## Fails when
- CSS `@media` or JavaScript forces a single orientation without an essential reason
- Content becomes inaccessible or broken when the device is rotated
- A message says "Please rotate your device" with no alternative

## Why it matters
Users who have their devices mounted in a fixed orientation (e.g. wheelchair mounts) cannot rotate their device. Locking orientation excludes these users.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
