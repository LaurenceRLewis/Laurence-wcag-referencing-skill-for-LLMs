# SC 1.4.7 — Low or No Background Audio (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
For prerecorded audio-only content containing speech, background audio is absent, at least 20dB lower than the foreground speech, or can be turned off — so users with hearing difficulties can distinguish speech from background noise.

## Passes when
- No background audio in speech recordings
- Background audio is at least 20dB quieter than the speech (approximately 4× quieter in perceived volume)
- A control is provided to turn off background audio independently

## Exceptions
Audio CAPTCHAs and audio logos are exempt.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
