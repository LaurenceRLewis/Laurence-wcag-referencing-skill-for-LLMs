# SC 1.3.6 — Identify Purpose (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
The purpose of user interface components, icons, and regions can be programmatically determined, enabling personalisation tools to replace or supplement them with symbols, simpler language, or other adaptations.

## Passes when
- Landmarks, icons, and components have unambiguous programmatic roles and labels
- Icons are supplemented with accessible names that clearly convey their purpose
- Regions use appropriate landmark roles (`<main>`, `<nav>`, `<aside>`, etc.)

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
