# SC 1.4.5 — Images of Text (Level AA)

## Intent
Use actual text rather than images of text where the same visual presentation can be achieved with CSS, so text can be resized, reflowed, and customised by users.

## Passes when
- Body content, headings, and labels are real text styled with CSS
- Logotypes (brand names or product logos rendered in a specific font) are acceptable as images
- Images of text where the visual presentation is essential and cannot be replicated in CSS are acceptable

## Fails when
- Regular headings or body content are rendered as images of text when CSS could achieve the same appearance
- Stylised text in banners or promotional images where the text could instead be real text with CSS styling

## Exceptions
- Logotypes — brand images with text are exempt
- Essential presentations — where the specific rendering is essential to the information (e.g. a sample of a particular handwriting style)

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
