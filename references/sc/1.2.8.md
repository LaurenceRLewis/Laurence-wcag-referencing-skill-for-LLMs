# SC 1.2.8 — Media Alternative (Prerecorded) (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Provide a full text alternative for all prerecorded synchronised media and video-only media.

## Passes when
A complete text document is provided describing all audio and visual content in the media — equivalent to a full screenplay or transcript with visual descriptions.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
