# SC 3.1.4 — Abbreviations (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
A mechanism for identifying the expanded form or meaning of abbreviations is available.

## Passes when
- Abbreviations are expanded on first use: 'Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)'
- `<abbr title="Web Content Accessibility Guidelines">WCAG</abbr>` used

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
