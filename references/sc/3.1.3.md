# SC 3.1.3 — Unusual Words (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
A mechanism is available for identifying specific definitions of unusual or specialist terms, jargon, and idioms.

## Passes when
- A glossary is provided and linked from technical terms
- Terms are linked to their definitions
- Definitions are provided inline

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
