# SC 1.2.4 — Captions (Live) (Level AA)

## Intent
Provide captions for all live audio content in synchronised media.

## Applies to
Live video with audio — live streams, webinars, broadcasts, live events.

## Passes when
Real-time captions are provided via CART (Communication Access Realtime Translation) or equivalent live captioning service.

## Fails when
- No captions provided for live audio content
- Captions are delayed significantly (pre-prepared captions for live content that don't match the live audio)

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
