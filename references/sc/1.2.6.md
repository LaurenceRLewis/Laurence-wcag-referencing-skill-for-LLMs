# SC 1.2.6 — Sign Language (Prerecorded) (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Provide sign language interpretation for all prerecorded audio content in synchronised media, for users whose primary language is a sign language.

## Passes when
A sign language interpretation video is provided alongside the main media content.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
