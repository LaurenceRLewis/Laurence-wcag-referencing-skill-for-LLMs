# SC 1.4.9 — Images of Text (No Exception) (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Images of text are used only for pure decoration or where the presentation is essential. Stricter than SC 1.4.5 — logotypes are not exempt at this level.

## Passes when
Images of text are used only where:
- The image is purely decorative with no informational value, OR
- The specific visual rendering is essential to the information conveyed

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.

## Related criteria
- SC 1.4.5 — Images of Text (Level AA — allows logotypes)
