# SC 1.4.6 — Contrast (Enhanced) (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Stricter contrast requirement than SC 1.4.3, providing better readability for users with more significant low vision.

## Requirements

| Text type | Minimum contrast ratio |
|---|---|
| Normal text (< 18pt / < 14pt bold) | 7:1 |
| Large text (≥ 18pt or ≥ 14pt bold) | 4.5:1 |

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.

## Related criteria
- SC 1.4.3 — Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA — the standard requirement)
