# SC 3.1.5 — Reading Level (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
When text requires more than a lower secondary education reading level, a supplemental version or visual aid is provided.

## Passes when
- A plain-language summary or alternative version is provided
- Illustrations or diagrams supplement complex text

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
