# SC 1.2.2 — Captions (Prerecorded) (Level A)

## Intent
Provide captions for all prerecorded audio content in synchronised media so deaf and hard-of-hearing users can access the information.

## Applies to
Prerecorded video with audio (films, recorded webinars, tutorials with narration).

## Passes when
- Captions are synchronised with the audio
- Captions include all dialogue and identify speakers where needed
- Captions include relevant non-speech audio (music cues, sound effects, laughter)
- Captions are accurate — not auto-generated without correction

## Fails when
- No captions provided
- Auto-generated captions used without review and correction
- Captions are not synchronised with the audio
- Captions omit significant non-speech sounds

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
