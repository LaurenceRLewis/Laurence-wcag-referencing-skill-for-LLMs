# SC 2.3.2 — Three Flashes (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Content does not flash more than 3 times per second — stricter than SC 2.3.1 because the threshold exception does not apply.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
