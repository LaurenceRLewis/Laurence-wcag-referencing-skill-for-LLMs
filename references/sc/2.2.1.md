# SC 2.2.1 — Timing Adjustable (Level A)

## Intent
For any time limit set by the content, users can turn off, adjust (at least 10× the default), or extend the limit — so users who need more time can complete tasks without losing their work.

## Passes when
- Time limits can be turned off before they begin, OR
- Time limit can be extended to at least 10× the default, OR
- User is warned before the limit expires (at least 20 seconds notice) and given a simple mechanism to extend it — at least 10 times

## Fails when
- Session times out without warning or opportunity to extend
- A form submission timer runs out with no extension option
- A quiz enforces a time limit with no adjustment mechanism

## Exceptions
- Real-time events (live auctions, live broadcasts) where the time limit is essential
- Time limits essential to the activity (a timed test where the time limit cannot be removed)
- Time limits longer than 20 hours

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
