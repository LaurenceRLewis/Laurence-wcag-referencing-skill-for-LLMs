# SC 1.2.1 — Audio-only and Video-only (Prerecorded) (Level A)

## Intent
Provide alternatives for prerecorded audio-only and video-only content so users who cannot hear or see the media can access the information.

## Applies to
- Prerecorded audio-only content (podcasts, audio clips)
- Prerecorded video-only content (silent animations, silent video tours)

Does not apply to synchronised media (video with audio — see SC 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.5).

## Passes when
- **Audio-only:** A text transcript is provided containing all spoken content and descriptions of relevant non-speech sounds
- **Video-only:** Either a text transcript or an audio track describing all visual content is provided

## Fails when
- A podcast or audio clip has no transcript
- A silent product tour video has no text description or audio narration alternative

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
