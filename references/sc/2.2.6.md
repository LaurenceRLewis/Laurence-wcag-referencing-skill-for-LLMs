# SC 2.2.6 — Timeouts (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Users are warned of the duration of any user inactivity that will cause data loss, unless the data is preserved for more than 20 hours of inactivity.

## Passes when
- Users are warned upfront how long they have before inactivity causes data loss, OR
- Data is preserved for more than 20 hours of inactivity

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.

## Related criteria
- SC 2.2.1 — Timing Adjustable
