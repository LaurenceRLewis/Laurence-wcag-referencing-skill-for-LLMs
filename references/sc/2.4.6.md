# SC 2.4.6 — Headings and Labels (Level AA)

## Intent
Headings and labels describe the topic or purpose of the content or control they introduce — so users can understand what a section or form field is about before reading its content.

## Passes when
- Headings accurately describe the section they introduce
- Form labels clearly identify the purpose of their input

## Fails when
- Generic headings: "Section 1", "Part A", "More information"
- Generic labels: "Field 1", "Input", "Enter value"
- Heading text does not match the content of the section

## Note
This SC does not require headings or labels to be present — SC 1.3.1 and SC 3.3.2 cover that. This SC only requires that headings and labels are *descriptive* when they exist.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
