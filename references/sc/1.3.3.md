# SC 1.3.3 — Sensory Characteristics (Level A)

## Intent
Instructions for operating content do not rely solely on sensory characteristics — shape, colour, size, visual location, orientation, or sound — so users who cannot perceive those characteristics can still follow the instructions.

## Applies to
Any instructional content that references UI elements by their appearance or position alone.

## Passes when
Instructions include non-sensory identifiers alongside any sensory references:
- "Click the Submit button" (not "click the green button")
- "Use the search field at the top of the page labelled Search" (not "the field in the top right")

## Fails when
- "Click the round icon on the right" with no other identifier
- "See the red items in the list" as the only way to identify errors
- "Press the large button" with no label reference

## Note
This SC covers all sensory characteristics. SC 1.4.1 covers colour specifically.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
