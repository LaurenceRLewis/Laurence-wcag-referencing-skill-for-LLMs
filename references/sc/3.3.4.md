# SC 3.3.4 — Error Prevention (Legal, Financial, Data) (Level AA)

## Intent
For pages that cause legal commitments, financial transactions, or modify/delete user-controlled data, submissions are reversible, checked for errors, or confirmed — so users can avoid and recover from serious mistakes.

## Applies to
- E-commerce checkout and purchase confirmation
- Account deletion
- Form submissions that create legal records
- Data deletion or irreversible changes
- Financial transactions

## The three ways to satisfy this
At least one of:
1. **Reversible** — submission can be reversed (cancel or modify an order after submitting)
2. **Checked** — data is checked for input errors and the user can correct before final submission
3. **Confirmed** — a confirmation step reviews the information before final commitment

## Passes when
- Order confirmation page before final purchase with ability to go back and change
- "Are you sure?" dialog before deleting an account
- Form checks for errors and highlights them before allowing submission

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
