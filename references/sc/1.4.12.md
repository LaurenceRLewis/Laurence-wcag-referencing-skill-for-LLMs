# SC 1.4.12 — Text Spacing (Level AA)

## Intent
Content and functionality are preserved when users override text spacing properties — so users who need increased spacing for readability can apply their preferences without losing content.

## The test
Apply ALL of the following simultaneously and verify no content or functionality is lost:
- Line height: at least 1.5× the font size
- Spacing after paragraphs: at least 2× the font size
- Letter spacing: at least 0.12em
- Word spacing: at least 0.16em

## Passes when
All content remains readable and all functionality is operable after applying the above spacing values. No text is clipped, overlapping, or hidden.

## Fails when
- Fixed-height containers clip text when line height increases
- `overflow: hidden` hides content when spacing increases
- Absolute-positioned elements overlap reflowed text
- Buttons or labels lose their text

## Test method
Use a bookmarklet or browser extension that applies the spacing values, then visually inspect for clipped or overlapping content.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
