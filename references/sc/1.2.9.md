# SC 1.2.9 — Audio-only (Live) (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Provide an alternative for live audio-only content — such as a live text stream or real-time transcript.

## Passes when
A text stream or real-time captioning is provided alongside live audio-only content (e.g. live radio, live audio broadcast).

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
