# SC 2.3.1 — Three Flashes or Below Threshold (Level A)

## Intent
Content does not contain anything that flashes more than 3 times per second, or the flash is below the general flash and red flash thresholds — to prevent triggering seizures in users with photosensitive epilepsy.

## Passes when
- No content flashes more than 3 times per second, OR
- Flashing content is below the general flash threshold (small area, low contrast) or red flash threshold

## Fails when
- Animated GIFs, videos, or JavaScript animations flash rapidly (> 3 Hz) in a large area
- Strobe effects used without warning or control

## Test method
Use the Photosensitive Epilepsy Analysis Tool (PEAT) or equivalent to measure flash frequency and area.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
