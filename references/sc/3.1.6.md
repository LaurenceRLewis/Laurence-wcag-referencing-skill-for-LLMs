# SC 3.1.6 — Pronunciation (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
A mechanism for identifying the pronunciation of words is available where meaning is ambiguous without pronunciation.

## Passes when
- Pronunciation guide provided where words are ambiguous
- Audio pronunciation or phonetic spelling available

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
