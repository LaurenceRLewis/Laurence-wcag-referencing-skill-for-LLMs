# SC 1.4.2 — Audio Control (Level A)

## Intent
If any audio plays automatically for more than 3 seconds, a mechanism must be available to pause, stop, or control the volume independently of the system volume.

## Applies to
Auto-playing audio, background music, auto-playing video with audio.

## Passes when
- Auto-playing audio has a visible pause or stop control reachable at the top of the page
- Volume control is provided independent of system volume
- The control is keyboard accessible

## Fails when
- Background audio plays automatically with no control to stop it
- The stop/pause control is not keyboard accessible
- Audio plays for more than 3 seconds without any user control

## Why it matters
Auto-playing audio interferes with screen reader speech and is disorienting for users with cognitive disabilities.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
