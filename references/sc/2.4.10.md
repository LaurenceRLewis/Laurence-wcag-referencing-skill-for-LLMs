# SC 2.4.10 — Section Headings (Level AAA)

> **Level AAA — for reference only.** Not required for standard AA conformance.

## Intent
Section headings are used to organise content — so users can understand the structure and navigate by heading.

## Passes when
Each distinct section of content is introduced by a heading that describes its topic.

## Note
This SC applies to headings within content, not just page-level structure. It requires headings to be used where content has distinct sections, even within a single page.

## WCAG 2.2 change
No change from WCAG 2.1.
